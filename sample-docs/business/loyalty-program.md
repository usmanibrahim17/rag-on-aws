# Glimmerwick Gadgets - Wick Points Loyalty Program

Glimmerwick Gadgets is a fictional company invented for this demo.

## How points work

You earn 1 Wick Point for every $1 you spend on products. Points are not earned on shipping fees, taxes or gift cards. Every 100 points can be exchanged for a $5 reward voucher. Vouchers are applied at checkout and cannot be combined with other discount codes.

## Tiers

- Spark: 0 to 499 points earned in the last 12 months. Standard benefits.
- Glow: 500 to 1,499 points. You also receive early access to new products.
- Blaze: 1,500 points or more. You also receive free express shipping on every order.

## Birthday bonus

Members receive 50 bonus points in their birthday month. Add your birthday to your account at least 30 days in advance to receive it.

## Expiry

Points expire after 18 months without any purchase. Any new purchase resets the 18-month period for all your points. Reward vouchers are valid for 90 days.

## Joining

Joining is free. Create a Glimmerwick account and you are enrolled automatically. Points from past orders are added within 48 hours of delivery.
