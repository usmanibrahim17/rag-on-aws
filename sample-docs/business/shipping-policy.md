# Glimmerwick Gadgets - Shipping Policy

Glimmerwick Gadgets is a fictional company invented for this demo.

## Shipping options and prices

- Standard shipping takes 4 to 6 business days and costs $5.90. It is free for orders over $60.
- Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.50. Express is not available for PO boxes.
- We do not offer same-day delivery.

## Dispatch times

Orders placed before 14:00 UTC on a business day are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 14:00 UTC, or on a weekend or public holiday, are dispatched on the next business day. We do not ship on weekends.

## International shipping

We ship to the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom only. International orders take 7 to 12 business days and cost a flat $18. Customers in Canada and the United Kingdom are responsible for any customs duties and import taxes charged on arrival. We cannot ship to other countries.

## Lost or delayed parcels

If your parcel has not arrived 10 business days after dispatch, contact support with your order number. We open an investigation with the carrier and, if the parcel cannot be found, we send a replacement free of charge or refund you in full, whichever you prefer.

## Damaged on arrival

If an item arrives damaged, take a photo of the box and the product and email it to support within 7 days of delivery. We send a replacement at no cost and arrange the collection of the damaged item.

## Delivery address

We deliver to the address entered at checkout. You can change it within 2 hours of placing the order. We cannot redirect a parcel once it has been dispatched.
