# Glimmerwick Gadgets - Customer Support FAQ

Glimmerwick Gadgets is a fictional company invented for this demo. Nothing here describes a real business.

## About us

Glimmerwick Gadgets was founded in 2019 in the fictional town of Lowmarsh. We make three products: the Aurora Lamp (a smart LED desk lamp), the Pebble Charger (a wireless phone charger) and the Nimbus Plug (a Wi-Fi smart plug).

## How do I contact support?

Our support team answers Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 UTC. You can email support@glimmerwick.example or call +1 555-0142. We reply to emails within one business day. We do not offer weekend support.

## How do I reset my Aurora Lamp?

Hold the power button for 10 seconds until the lamp blinks blue three times. This clears the Wi-Fi settings and restores factory defaults. After the reset, pair the lamp again from the Glimmerwick app.

## Can I change or cancel my order?

You can change the delivery address or cancel an order within 2 hours of placing it. After that the order goes to the warehouse and can no longer be changed. To cancel after the 2 hours have passed, wait for delivery and return the item under our returns policy.

## How do I track my order?

A tracking email with a link is sent within 24 hours of dispatch. If you cannot find it, check your spam folder or ask support to resend it.

## Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards, bank transfer and Glimmerwick gift cards. We do not accept cash on delivery.

## Do gift cards expire?

No. Glimmerwick gift cards never expire and have no fees. A gift card cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Can I use the Aurora Lamp without the app?

Yes. The touch panel on the base switches the lamp on and off and cycles through three brightness levels. The app is only needed for schedules and colour temperature presets.

## Where can I find the serial number?

The serial number is printed on a label under the base of each product and on the invoice email. You need it for warranty claims.
