# Glimmerwick Gadgets - Product Care and Troubleshooting

Glimmerwick Gadgets is a fictional company invented for this demo.

## Aurora Lamp

### The lamp flickers

Flickering is almost always caused by a power adapter that is not the one supplied. Use only the 12V adapter from the box. If the lamp still flickers with the original adapter, the LED module may be faulty. Contact support: the LED module is covered by the extended warranty and is replaced free of charge.

### Cleaning

Unplug the lamp and wipe it with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use water, glass cleaner or solvents on the diffuser, because they can cloud the surface.

## Pebble Charger

### The charger light is amber

An amber light means the charger detected a foreign object between the phone and the pad, usually a coin, a key or a metal phone case. Remove the object or the metal case and the light turns white again.

### Charging speed

The Pebble Charger delivers up to 15W. Thick cases over 5 mm reduce the speed or stop charging altogether. Remove the case if charging is slow.

## Nimbus Plug

### Pairing the plug

Hold the button on the plug for 5 seconds until the light blinks quickly, then add the plug in the Glimmerwick app. The plug connects to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks only. It cannot join a 5 GHz network, so make sure your phone is on the 2.4 GHz band while pairing.

### Maximum load

The Nimbus Plug supports devices up to 10A (2300W). Do not connect heaters or other high-power appliances above this limit.
