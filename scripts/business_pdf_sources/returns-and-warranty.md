# Glimmerwick Gadgets - Returns and Warranty Policy

Glimmerwick Gadgets is a fictional company invented for this demo.

## Returns

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The item must be unused and in its original packaging. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item.

Return shipping costs $6.50, deducted from the refund. If the item is defective or we sent the wrong one, return shipping is free.

Items that cannot be returned:
- Gift cards.
- Engraved or personalised lamps.
- Items marked Final Sale at checkout.

## Exchanges

You can exchange an item for a different colour or model once, within 30 days of delivery. If the new item costs more you pay the difference. If it costs less we refund the difference.

## Warranty coverage

All Glimmerwick products are covered against manufacturing defects:
- Aurora Lamp: 3 years on the LED module and 1 year on all other parts.
- Pebble Charger: 2 years.
- Nimbus Plug: 2 years.

The warranty starts on the delivery date. It is not transferable to a second owner.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover water damage, damage from using the wrong power adapter, repairs made by anyone other than Glimmerwick, or normal cosmetic wear such as scratches.

## Making a warranty claim

Email support@glimmerwick.example with your order number, the serial number from the label under the base, and a photo or short video showing the problem. Once the claim is approved we ship a replacement within 3 business days. You do not need to send the faulty item back unless we ask for it.
