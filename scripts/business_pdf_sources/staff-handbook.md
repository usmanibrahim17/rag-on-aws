# Glimmerwick Gadgets - Staff Handbook

Glimmerwick Gadgets is a fictional company invented for this demo. This handbook is for employees.

## Working hours and remote work

The standard working week is 37.5 hours. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 UTC, when everyone should be reachable. After the 3-month probation period, employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with their manager's agreement.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per year, in addition to public holidays. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year and must be used by March 31. Leave requests need manager approval at least 2 weeks in advance for absences longer than 5 days.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A medical note is required after 3 consecutive sick days. Tell your manager before your start time on the first day of absence.

## Parental leave

The primary carer receives 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary carer receives 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave can start up to 4 weeks before the expected birth or adoption date.

## Expenses and travel

Submit expense claims within 30 days. Receipts are required for any single item over $25. Travel is booked in economy class, and hotel stays are limited to $140 per night. Claims are paid in the next monthly payroll.

## Learning budget

Every employee has a learning budget of $800 per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Equipment

Employees receive a laptop on their first day. Laptops are replaced every 3 years. Report lost or stolen equipment to IT within 24 hours.

## Notice period and contact

After probation the notice period is 1 month for both the employee and the company. Questions about this handbook go to people@glimmerwick.example. Concerns can also be reported anonymously through the confidential line at +1 555-0177.
